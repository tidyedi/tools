Convention importer

Upload a DLA/DLMS-style implementation convention PDF. It's parsed -- not stored -- into its segment table (Pos/Id/Name/Req/Max Use/Repeat/ Notes/Usage) and, per segment, every element's definition and code meanings, straight from the document that defines them.

Nothing you upload is stored, logged, or sent anywhere. The PDF is parsed in memory and forgotten once the response is returned.

Best-effort text-layout parsing of a real document, not a guaranteed-correct one: this was built and tested against DLA's own IC PDFs (which share one consistent generator format). A convention from a different publisher or generator may parse only partially, or not at all -- rows that don't match an expected shape are skipped rather than guessed at.
